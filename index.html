Form pattern examples (OSIS, Password, Credit Card)

Part 1 — OSIS Number
Type exactly 8 digits (0-9). No letters or spaces.
Part 2 — Password
Needs: 1 uppercase, 1 lowercase, 1 number, minimum 8 characters.
Part 3 — Credit Card Number
